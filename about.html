About us

Hello, we're Le Bean.

A small coffee and comfort food spot in Greenwoods, Mintal, Davao City, built on one simple idea: a good cup can make an ordinary day better.

Sip. Smile. Repeat.It's our tagline, our routine, and our promise.

(01)Our story

Small shop, big heart.

Le Bean started with a simple counter, a coffee machine, and a lot of test lattes. Word spread, regulars kept coming back, and our menu grew to include the Filipino rice meals our neighbours love.

Today, we still make every order the way we did on day one — with a smile.
