When can I order?

Monday 7am – 7pm and 9pm – 11:59pm, Tuesday to Saturday 24 hours, and Sunday 12am – 3pm. Yes, we're open all night for your late cravings.

Delivery range is set by GrabFood based on your location. If Le Bean shows up in your app, we can reach you.

They're GrabFood recognitions. 5-Star Eats comes from customer ratings, and Top Service means we're consistently available, on time, and get orders right.
